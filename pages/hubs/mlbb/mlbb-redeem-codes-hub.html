How to find your MLBB Game ID: Open Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, tap your Avatar in the top-left corner of the lobby, and look at the numeric ID shown below your profile name (for example, 123456789 (1234)). Enter only the main Game ID before the parentheses unless the official form asks for the Server/Zone ID separately.

What rewards do official MLBB codes usually grant?

Legitimate Mobile Legends: Bang Bang promotional codes are typically distributed during esports broadcasts (such as MPL, MSC, or M-Series tournaments), seasonal updates, and regional livestreams. Common rewards include:

  • Hero & Skin Trial Cards: Time-limited passes (1-day, 3-day, or 7-day) to test featured heroes or elite/special skins.
  • Magic Dust, Fragments & Battle Points: Emblem upgrade materials, Hero Fragments, Premium Skin Fragments, and Double BP Cards.
  • Event Tokens & Emotes: Occasional tournament chests, avatar borders, or limited battle emotes.

Be cautious of any website claiming to offer unlimited free Diamonds through a generator—official codes rarely grant Diamonds directly and never require your account password.

Common redemption problems

  • Limit reached or expired: Many livestream and esports codes have strict global redemption caps that fill up within minutes of broadcast.
  • Region or server mismatch: Your account region or game version does not match the campaign’s target territory.
  • Already claimed: Promotional codes are limited to one redemption per MLBB Game ID.
  • Verification code delay: The one-time verification code sent to your in-game mail usually expires after 30 minutes—request a fresh code if the portal times out.

If a claim fails, verify the promotion on an official channel. Avoid pages that ask for your password or promise guaranteed diamonds.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the code list empty?

We don’t publish guessed or untraceable codes. This hub is ready for listings, and will be updated when a reliable source and publication date are available.

What details do I need to redeem an MLBB code?

You will normally need the active promotional code, your numeric MLBB Game ID from your profile page, and the verification code sent to your in-game mail inbox.

Can all regions use the same code?

Not necessarily. Check each publisher announcement for eligible regions and servers before attempting to redeem.

Where do claimed rewards appear?

Once the official redemption page confirms success, open Mobile Legends: Bang Bang and check your in-game Mail icon on the top-right of the lobby to claim the attached items.
