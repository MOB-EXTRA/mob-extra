Common redemption problems

  • The offer has expired or reached its claim limit.
  • Your account region or game version does not match the promotion.
  • The code was already used on your account.
  • Required verification has not arrived yet, or the publisher service is temporarily busy.

If a claim fails, verify the promotion on an official channel. Avoid pages that ask for your password or promise guaranteed diamonds.
