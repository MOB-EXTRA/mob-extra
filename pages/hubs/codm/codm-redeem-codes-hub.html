Global vs. Garena CODM codes explained

Call of Duty: Mobile operates under different regional publishers, which directly affects how promotional codes work:

  • Global Version (Activision): Covers North America, Europe, Latin America, India, and other global territories. Codes are redeemed on Activision’s web portal using your UID, the promotional code, and a 4-digit CAPTCHA verification number.
  • Garena Version (Southeast Asia): Covers the Philippines, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, and Taiwan. Garena codes are typically redeemed inside the in-game Events → Featured → Redemption Center tab rather than on Activision’s website.

Why a code may not work

  • Expired or fully claimed: promotional codes often have a fixed time window or a limited number of claims.
  • Region mismatch: a Garena code will not work on Global, and Global codes will reject Garena UIDs.
  • Already redeemed: many rewards can be claimed only once per account.
  • CAPTCHA or server congestion: during major season launches or championship streams, the redemption portal may fail to load verification images—refresh the CAPTCHA and remove any trailing spaces when pasting your code.

Do not enter your password on third-party pages that promise free rewards. MOB EXTRA does not request account credentials.

Frequently asked questions

Are these codes guaranteed to work?

No. We share their recorded source date and version, but cannot verify every account’s eligibility or guarantee that a time-limited offer remains active.

How do I find my Call of Duty: Mobile UID?

Tap your profile icon at the top-left of the lobby, open the Basic tab, and tap the copy icon next to your numeric UID below your avatar picture.

Where do claimed rewards arrive in-game?

Once confirmed by the redemption center, rewards are delivered to your in-game Mailbox (the envelope icon along the top bar of the main lobby).

Where can I find newly announced codes?

Check official Call of Duty: Mobile and Garena announcements first. We review the community source list and update this page when new entries are confirmed for publication.
