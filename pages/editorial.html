OUR UPDATE PROCESS

Useful information
needs its context.

This page explains how MOB EXTRA sources community updates, labels time-sensitive information, and handles corrections.

Sources and review

We prioritize publisher announcements and official game portals for rules, redemption routes, and platform instructions. Where a page reports a community-sourced code or test build link, it should say so rather than imply publisher verification. External sources are linked where practical, and readers should confirm time-sensitive details at the source.

Codes are checked against the source list available when a page is updated. A listing date tells when it was recorded, not a guarantee that the code remains active. Promotions may expire, become region-restricted, or reach a claim limit without notice.

Corrections and updates

When a page changes materially, we update its review date and clarify the change where it helps readers. If you spot a broken link, incorrect instruction, or an expired listing, please include the page URL and the specific item when you contact us.

Independent coverage

MOB EXTRA is a fan-made information resource. We do not represent game publishers and do not claim access to private promotional data. Reviews, comments, or third-party submissions may be moderated if interactive community features are introduced.
