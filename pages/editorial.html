OUR UPDATE PROCESS

Useful information
needs its context.

This page explains how MOB EXTRA gives context to game updates, describes tools and their limits, and handles corrections across the site.

Sources, context, and tools

For game rules, redemption routes, and platform instructions, we prioritize publisher announcements and official game portals. Community-sourced codes, updates, or test-build links should be identified as such rather than presented as publisher-verified. External sources are linked where practical, and readers should confirm time-sensitive details at the source.

For tools and converters, we aim to explain the purpose, inputs, and important assumptions so visitors can judge whether an output suits their task. Check results in the intended environment before relying on them for a project. Codes and promotions can expire, become region-restricted, or reach claim limits; a listing date is not a guarantee of current availability.

Video previews and social sources

The CODM name formatter shows a clickable thumbnail preview for a YouTube video; selecting it opens the video on YouTube rather than playing it on this site. We may also link to YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), TikTok, and other social or community sources. These services and their content are operated by third parties; a link is for reference and does not imply endorsement of every statement, post, or video. Content may change or be removed without notice. See our Privacy Policy for information about thumbnail requests and external links.

Corrections and updates

When a page changes materially, we update its review date and clarify the change where it helps readers. If you spot an incorrect instruction, unexpected tool result, outdated information, or broken link, include the page URL and the specific issue when you contact us.

Independent coverage and community reviews

MOB EXTRA is an independent collection of digital resources. We do not represent game publishers or claim access to private promotional data. Public review features are active: reviews are submitted by visitors, may appear immediately, and are not editorially verified or endorsements by MOB EXTRA. We may remove content that violates the Terms of Use. Do not include private or sensitive information in a review; see the Privacy Policy for how review data is handled.
