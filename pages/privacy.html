YOUR PRIVACY

Clear information
about data.

Effective date: November 19, 2026. This policy covers the MOB EXTRA website and its CODM test build hub.

Information handled by the site

MOB EXTRA does not require an account to read its pages or use its basic tools. If you contact us, we receive the email address and message you choose to send. Our static hosting provider processes ordinary server request data, which can include IP address, browser details, requested URL, and time, to deliver and protect the site. Google Fonts may receive standard connection information when your browser requests font files.

CODM test build hub and verification

The hub loads public status, build descriptions, links, and any current community access code from shared GitHub Pages JavaScript configuration repositories. This data is processed in your browser. The access code and comparison logic are visible to visitors and are not account authentication or a security boundary. Entering a code does not ask for or transmit your game credentials. The acknowledgement checkbox records no signature or identity. When you open a source or download link, that external provider receives your request under its own privacy policy.

Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager

The site loads Google Tag Manager container GTM-TZ7P5GLL. The container code can load Google Analytics or other tags according to its configuration; this repository does not reveal which tags are currently published inside the container. Google services may collect usage and device information, including page interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, identifiers, and cookie or similar storage data, depending on enabled tags and your settings. Review the live container configuration and Google’s disclosures to confirm actual collection. You can manage browser cookies and review Google’s controls through Google’s partner site information and Google Analytics opt-out.

The Google site verification meta tag verifies site ownership to Google Search Console. It does not itself collect visitor data. The site is hosted as static files through GitHub Pages; it is not embedded in Google Sites. If we change hosting or add Google Sites content, this notice will be revised to reflect the actual configuration.

Firebase and community reviews

Review widgets connect to Firebase Firestore using the Firebase JavaScript SDK. When you view or interact with those widgets, Firebase may process technical request information such as IP address, browser/device details, and data needed to retrieve or submit a review. Submitted reviews may include your optional display name, category, selected tool or hub (for utility reviews), rating, review text, the page or feature being reviewed (including its page path and title), and a browser-generated device token used to find or edit that device’s review for that page. The utility list is generated from the site’s sitemap and the current page is selected by default where possible. Reviews are available on MOB EXTRA and its owner-managed Vile Tempest and Marc Johnford portfolio pages; third-party portfolio pages do not accept reviews. Do not include passwords, account identifiers, or other sensitive information in a review. Firebase services are not used by the CODM test build verification gate. See Firebase privacy and security information.

Cookies, promotions, and advertising

MOB EXTRA’s local promotional cards are first-party site content. Their code does not itself load an advertising network or record promotion impressions or clicks. Google Tag Manager and any tags enabled through its container may use cookies or similar technologies as described above.

Google AdSense is not currently configured on this site: there is no AdSense publisher ID or ad-serving script in the site’s ad module. If AdSense or another advertising provider is added, its cookies, identifiers, data use, and any consent choices required for visitors’ regions will be described here before deployment. A privacy policy alone does not replace any required consent mechanism.

External services, children, and your choices

Links may lead to YouTube, GitHub, Google services, Apple TestFlight, game publisher sites, or other providers. Those providers control their own data practices. MOB EXTRA is a general gaming information site and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact us about information a child has sent.

You may block cookies in your browser, though some third-party features may not work as intended. For privacy questions or requests, email mob.extra.contact@gmail.com. We will update this policy when relevant services or practices change.
