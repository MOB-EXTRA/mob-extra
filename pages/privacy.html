Effective date: November 28, 2026. This policy covers the MOB EXTRA website, its shared review features, and the owner-managed portfolio pages that use those features. Page-specific notices may add to this policy.

Information you provide

You do not need an account to browse the site. The Contact page builds an email draft in your browser and does not submit or store the form fields on MOB EXTRA. Your email application/provider handles the draft; we receive the message only if you send it. Do not include passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information in an inquiry.

Review submissions are sent to Firebase Firestore and may be published immediately in a public feed. A review may include an optional display name, category, selected feature, rating, text, page path/title, and submission time. The review system also creates a random browser token, stores it in local storage, and includes it with the review so the same browser can find or edit its review. This token is not an account credential or a secret; do not rely on it to protect sensitive information. Reviews and display names are public and may be copied, indexed, or cached by others. You can request removal by email; there is no self-service delete control.

Site technology and service providers

The site is served as static files through GitHub Pages. MOB EXTRA pages also request public data files from GitHub Pages repositories used for shared site data. GitHub and delivery providers may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser/device details, requested URL, referrer, and time; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Google Fonts and the jsDelivr content delivery network may receive connection information when your browser requests their assets.

Google Tag Manager container GTM-TZ7P5GLL is loaded across the site. Google Analytics page-view traffic has been observed from the current configuration; tags and settings in the container can change without a website code change. Google services may process page interactions, device/browser information, approximate location, identifiers, and cookie or similar storage data. See Google’s partner site information, Google’s Privacy Policy, and the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Review data is handled by Firebase Firestore; see Firebase privacy information. Vile Tempest Official portfolio pages additionally call CounterAPI V2 to increment a page-view total. CounterAPI receives those requests and may process standard connection metadata; its collection and retention practices are controlled by that provider. The site does not control the privacy practices of these providers or of external destinations you choose to visit.

Video previews and social links

The CODM name formatter shows a clickable YouTube thumbnail preview; it does not load a YouTube player on MOB EXTRA. When the thumbnail loads, your browser requests an image from YouTube’s image host, which may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, browser/device details, and the referring site origin. Selecting the preview opens the video’s YouTube watch page, or may open the YouTube app depending on your device and settings. YouTube then handles viewing activity, any advertisements, and related data under its own terms and privacy practices. See Google’s Privacy Policy, YouTube’s Terms of Service, and YouTube’s video sharing information.

MOB EXTRA pages and portfolios may also link to YouTube, Reddit, Facebook/Meta, X (formerly Twitter), TikTok, and other services. Following a link takes you to that provider, which may receive connection information and information about your activity there. Those services set their own terms and privacy practices: Reddit, Facebook/Meta, X, and TikTok. We do not control their services or endorse every item they publish. Other social feeds are not currently embedded on the main site; if that changes, this notice will be updated.

Browser storage and purposes

Browser storage is used for site preferences such as theme choice, the review token and optional review name, temporary catalog caching, and a preview-store cart. The cart is a demonstration feature: the site does not process orders or payments. Some storage is necessary for the requested feature; clearing browser storage may reset preferences or prevent the review widget from finding a prior review. Google tags and third-party services may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their configuration and policies.

We use information to display and maintain pages and tools, fetch public data, publish and manage reviews, respond to messages you send, measure site use, protect the service, and meet applicable obligations. Providers may process information to deliver their services under their own terms.

Retention and your choices

Public reviews may remain available until removed by the site operator or the underlying service. Email correspondence is retained in the recipient’s email systems for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a project, keep necessary records, or meet legal obligations. Hosting, analytics, Firebase, and counter-service logs are subject to the providers’ settings and retention practices. Contact us to request access, correction, or removal of information we control; requests are handled subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

You can manage or clear browser storage and cookies through your browser. For Google Analytics, the opt-out add-on linked above is available. Applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights depending on where you live.

Advertising status and consent

The current first-party promotional cards link to MOB EXTRA content and are not Google AdSense units or a third-party ad network. No AdSense publisher ID or ad-serving script is present in this repository; the remotely configured Tag Manager container may change independently. If Google or another ad provider is enabled in the future, third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on previous visits to this or other sites; Google and its partners may personalize ads based on those visits. You can manage Google personalization through Google Ads Settings and industry opt-outs through YourAdChoices. The active vendor list and applicable notices must be updated before new ad vendors are enabled.

This site does not currently provide its own consent-management platform or record consent preferences. A policy is not a substitute for consent. Before deploying or continuing tags that require prior consent in a visitor’s jurisdiction, their behavior and consent controls must be configured accordingly. Before serving personalized ads in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

Children, security, and contact

The site is intended for a general audience and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you are below the age at which you may consent to online services where you live, ask a parent or guardian before submitting a review or contacting us. A parent or guardian may contact us about information a child has submitted. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure; avoid posting private information in public reviews.
