Effective date: November 18, 2026. This policy describes the current MOB EXTRA website and should be reviewed when its services or data practices change.

Information this site handles

The current site is primarily static. It does not ask visitors to create an account or submit profile information. If you email us, we receive the address and message you choose to send, and use them to respond or review a correction. Your browser may store local preferences needed for a tool or page to work. The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser, and requested page as part of delivering and protecting the site. The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts, so a browser may request font files from Google and disclose standard connection information such as IP address and browser details to that provider.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Some pages display sponsored promotions for MOB EXTRA services and tools. These promotions are served from files on this site and link to MOB EXTRA pages. The promotion code does not load a third-party advertising network, set advertising cookies, or track ad views or clicks. The hosting provider may still process ordinary requests when a promotion or destination page is loaded.

No third-party ad network is currently enabled. If an external advertising service such as Google AdSense is added later, that service and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve, measure, or personalize ads. This policy and any required consent controls will be updated before that service is enabled. Visitors can manage Google advertising personalization in Google Ads Settings and learn more about Google’s data use on Google partner sites.

External websites

Our pages link to game publishers, video platforms, and other third-party services. Those sites have their own privacy policies and data practices. Review them before sharing information or installing software.

Children and contact

MOB EXTRA is a general gaming information site and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information may contact us to request review or deletion where applicable.

For privacy questions, email mob.extra.contact@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the site changes; the effective date above will reflect revisions.
