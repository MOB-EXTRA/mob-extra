2. Privacy notice

Information you choose to send

The project inquiry form asks for your name, email address, service type, and project message. It does not submit those fields to a portfolio database. When you press “Prepare email,” your browser opens your email application with those details in a draft. The information is sent to Marc only if you choose to send that email.

If you email Marc directly, the email address, message, attachments, and any other information you include may be used to reply, assess a project, deliver agreed services, and keep necessary project or accounting records.

How information is used and retained

Inquiry information is used to respond to you and, if you proceed, to communicate about the project, prepare and administer an agreement, provide the work, and maintain records. If an inquiry does not become a project, the message is normally deleted within 12 months of the last meaningful contact unless a dispute or legal obligation requires longer retention. Project correspondence and invoices may be kept for the project and for as long as reasonably needed for accounting, support, dispute handling, or legal obligations, then deleted or securely disposed of where practicable.

Hosting and third-party requests

The site is made from static files. The hosting or delivery provider serving this portfolio may process technical request data, such as IP address, browser, device, requested files, and timestamps, in its operational logs. The site also requests fonts from Google Fonts and loads the Vile Tempest Official favicon from its published site. Those providers receive ordinary connection data when your browser requests those files. Social and project links take you to services with their own privacy practices; this portfolio does not control those services.

Your choices and privacy rights

You may choose not to submit an inquiry. Depending on applicable law, you may ask to be informed about or access personal information held about you, request correction, object to or restrict certain processing, request erasure or blocking where allowed, request portability where applicable, or raise a complaint. Write to the contact address above; requests are handled subject to identity checks, applicable law, and legitimate recordkeeping needs. If you are in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 describes these rights and complaint channels; see the National Privacy Commission’s Data Privacy Act reference and its data subject rights guidance.

Security and international processing

Email and internet transmissions can involve providers and systems in more than one country. No online transmission or storage method can be promised to be completely secure. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or sensitive personal information in a project inquiry.
