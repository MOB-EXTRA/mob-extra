MOB EXTRA is an independent gaming resource bringing together useful projects for mobile players. The site collects practical guides, community-tracked updates, and small utilities in one navigable place.

Why MOB EXTRA exists

Players often have to jump between scattered posts, outdated links, and tools that are hard to use on a phone. MOB EXTRA is being built as a central site for the resources we maintain across mobile games, starting with Call of Duty: Mobile guides and redeem information, then expanding to other games such as Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.

Our goal is to make every page understandable on its own: tell visitors what the resource does, show when information was reviewed, explain limitations, and point to the safest available source.

Independent and transparent

MOB EXTRA is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Activision, TiMi Studio Group, Tencent, Moonton, Garena, or other game publishers. Game names and trademarks identify the games discussed and remain the property of their respective owners.

Community-reported promotions and links can change. We label dates and provide context where possible, and we welcome corrections from visitors.
