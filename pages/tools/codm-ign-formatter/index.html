How to Change Your Name in Call of Duty: Mobile (via Inventory)

If you already own a Rename Card—or want to use the card directly without getting stuck on the basic profile edit screen—use the Loadout → Inventory method in the CODM lobby.

Step-by-Step: Rename Through Inventory (Recommended)

  1. Copy your formatted name first: Type your IGN in the formatter above, choose your preferred Unicode spacing character, and tap Copy name.
  2. Open Loadout in the lobby: From the Call of Duty: Mobile main lobby screen, tap Loadout in the bottom navigation bar (do not open your top-left Basic Player Profile).
  3. Open your Inventory: Look at the top-right area of the Loadout screen and tap the Inventory (backpack/crate) icon.
  4. Select your Rename Card: Find the Rename Card in your item list and tap Use.
  5. Paste and confirm: Tap the name input field in the Rename Card prompt, paste your copied IGN, verify that all characters render properly without missing boxes, and tap Change.

Why Use Inventory Instead of Basic Profile?

  • Direct item activation: Editing through Player Profile → Basic → Edit Name prompts you to buy a Rename Card even when you prefer activating an existing card from your bag.
  • Quick confirmation of available cards: Opening Inventory immediately shows how many Rename Cards you currently hold before you paste your formatted text.
  • Buying a Rename Card with Credits (C) instead of CP: If you do not have a Rename Card yet, open the in-game Store → Items (or tap Buy on the card prompt) and switch the currency toggle from CP to Credits (C) to purchase one with free earned Credits.

Comparing Unicode Spacing Characters for CODM

Standard keyboard spaces (U+0020) are often trimmed or rejected in Call of Duty: Mobile IGN fields. Each preset in this tool inserts a distinct Unicode whitespace or filler character:

Preset Name Unicode Code Point Visual Width & Behavior Best Use Case
Hangul filler U+3164 Full-width invisible Hangul character Most popular choice for clean visible gaps between clan tags and IGNs
Non-breaking space U+00A0 Standard space width without line break Subtle single-space separation that looks closest to normal typing
Braille blank U+2800 Empty Braille pattern cell Alternative invisible separator if another blank character is filtered
En quad U+2000 Half-em typographic spacing Balanced medium gap for stylized symbols and short handles

CODM Name Rules & Troubleshooting Tips

  • 14-character limit: CODM enforces a strict 14-character cap on player names, and every invisible Unicode separator counts as 1 character toward that total.
  • Rename cooldown: After changing your IGN, Call of Duty: Mobile applies a 7-day cooldown before you can use another Rename Card.
  • "Invalid character" or tofu boxes (□): Because Garena and Global updates periodically adjust allowed Unicode ranges, test a different spacing preset (such as switching from Hangul filler to Braille blank or Non-breaking space) if the game rejects your pasted name.
  • Local browser formatting: Your text is formatted directly inside your browser—nothing you type is uploaded or stored on external servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Inventory located in CODM to use a Rename Card?

From the main lobby, tap Loadout at the bottom of the screen, then tap the Inventory icon in the top-right corner. Locate your Rename Card inside the inventory grid and tap Use.

Can I get a CODM Rename Card without spending CP?

Yes. In the in-game Store under the Items tab, select the Rename Card and switch the purchase currency from COD Points (CP) to Credits (C) so you can buy it with free in-game Credits.

Why does my formatted name show fewer than 14 visible letters?

Invisible spacing characters like Hangul filler (U+3164) and Braille blank (U+2800) still occupy 1 character slot each. If you add two invisible spaces around a clan tag, you have 12 characters remaining for letters and symbols.

How often can I change my name in Call of Duty: Mobile?

You can change your name once every 7 days, provided you have a Rename Card in your Inventory.
